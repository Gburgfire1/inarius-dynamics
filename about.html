Built for the people who run toward the sirens.

Simplify everything from pre-hospital to hospital care — so responders can save lives and property, not drown in paperwork.

Inarius Dynamics was created by two firefighters working in one of the busiest jurisdictions in the world. On the busiest nights we kept asking the same questions: Why is this so difficult? Why is the system so fragmented? Why are we writing vitals on our gloves and bouncing between two to four different applications just to close out a single call?

We realized there had to be a better way — and Inarius Dynamics was born.

Our mission is to simplify everything about pre-hospital to hospital care. We reduce mental strain and overhead so responders can do what they were meant to do: save lives and property. They were never meant to spend endless hours on paperwork that drives fatigue and burnout.

At our core, we believe in an intelligent, vertically integrated system that runs locally — no internet required — so diagnostics and charting work anywhere, with total flexibility. Edge AI on-device. Offline-first. Built by the frontline, for the frontline.
