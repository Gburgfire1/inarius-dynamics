About

We build AI applications that earn a place in the workday.

Inarius Dynamics uses AI to build custom applications and solutions for businesses and industries that want higher productivity, tighter operations, and room to grow.

Inarius Dynamics builds software people run in the workday. ShopLine, an AI phone receptionist for independent auto repair shops, is the first product. It answers the shop’s line, books appointments, handles approved questions, and keeps the lead when a person cannot pick up.

Other engagements start from a specific operation. We look at where staff lose time, where a customer waits, and where a tool already in use should be doing more of the work. Then we build the application, put it into daily use, and stay with it.

The standard is practical. An assistant should know whose rules it is following. An automation should remove a real step. A new application should be measurable in hours returned, cost taken out, or revenue the business no longer drops.

How we judge the work

Specific to the business

The information, the hours, the services, and the handoff rules come from the customer.

Honest about limits

If the system should not quote a final price, take a payment, or pretend to be a person, that boundary is part of the build.

Used after launch

Discover, build, deploy, and support. The useful version is the one staff and customers actually touch.
