Old systems still run important work.

Many corporations and government offices depend on software written decades ago. It is hard to change, hard to staff, and hard to connect to anything new. The records inside it are often harder to find than they should be.

Six kinds of work.

Codebase modernization

We rewrite legacy applications with current engineering practices, using AI to move faster without skipping review, testing, or documentation.

Digital repositories and data integration

We build digital repositories and tightly integrate data across systems and industries, so information lives in one reliable place instead of scattered silos.

Tool and industry integrations

We connect modern tools, industry systems, and AI to the software you already run, for greater intelligence and more efficient workflows.

AI search for legacy data

We build applications that let people search huge databases and file systems in plain language and find the record they need.

Engagement analytics

We build analytics that track how people use your systems and content, so you can see what is working and where to improve.

Lightweight, no-install delivery
