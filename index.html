First product

ShopLine answers the shop phone.

ShopLine is an AI phone receptionist for independent auto repair shops. The shop keeps its number and chooses which calls to forward: every call, after hours, or the ones nobody picked up. Callers can book, ask a question the shop has approved, or leave a message instead of hanging up.

  • Answers from that shop’s hours, services, and frequently asked questions.
  • Books an appointment on ShopLine’s calendar or the shop’s Google Calendar.
  • Takes a message and keeps the caller, so the lead is not lost.
  • Can bring a person at the shop onto the call.
  • Says it is an AI, and says the call is recorded.
Request a ShopLine demo Product details

What the shop controls

Hours, services, price ranges, and answers live in a dashboard the shop edits. ShopLine only quotes ranges the shop entered, and it says the final price comes after inspection. It does not take card numbers, and it does not promise a diagnosis or a completion time.

  • Calls, summaries, appointments, and customers in one place.
  • A confirmation text only when the shop turns texts on and the caller agrees.
