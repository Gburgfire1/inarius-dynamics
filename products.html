Product OmniApp / OmniHealth

Clinical Documentation & Fire Investigation Suite

Zero-latency field operations. On-device AI. Full NEMSIS 3.5 / NERIS compliance.

Built for iPad Pro M‑series silicon. OmniApp combines Qwen 2.5 LLM reasoning, MedGemma vision analysis, and WhisperKit voice transcription into a single offline‑first overlay. From AR scene capture to cryptographic chain of custody — everything runs locally, even in dead zones.

AI

Edge AI Orchestrator

Qwen 2.5 (LLM) + MedGemma (VLM) + WhisperKit running concurrently on Apple Unified Memory via MLX / Metal. KV cache serialization for near‑zero TTFT.
