ShopLine

An AI phone receptionist for auto repair shops

ShopLine answers a shop’s forwarded phone line, speaks from that shop’s hours, services, and answers, and books into a calendar. The shop keeps its own number. Forwarding can cover every call, after-hours calls, or calls nobody answered.

On the call

  • Greets the caller as an AI assistant for that shop and says the call is recorded.
  • Answers hours, services, and the questions the shop has written down.
  • Offers only the price ranges the shop entered, and says the final price comes after inspection.
  • Books an open slot, or saves a request the shop can confirm.
  • Takes a message with the caller’s details when it should not book.
  • Can transfer the call to a person at the shop.
  • If the caller objects to recording, the transcript and recording are not stored. A booking or message already captured is kept.

For the shop

  • A dashboard for today’s calls, upcoming appointments, and recent call summaries.
  • Each call can show the outcome, summary, customer, and transcript, unless recording was declined.
  • Appointments can be added or cancelled. Customers keep their phone number and vehicles.
  • The shop edits hours, services, answers, slot length, and the number used for a transfer.
  • Booking can use ShopLine’s calendar or Google Calendar.
  • A confirmation text goes out only after the shop enables texts and the caller agrees. Until then, texts are not offered.

Designed limits

These are part of the product, not fine print we hope you miss.

  • It does not take card numbers.
  • It does not invent a price, a diagnosis, or a completion time.
  • If someone asks whether they are talking to a person, it says it is an AI assistant for the shop.
  • The shop cannot rewrite the opening line that discloses the AI and the recording.
